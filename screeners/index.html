Practice like the pros. Learn how legendary investors find great stocks.

What is a Screener?

A stock screener is a tool that helps you filter stocks based on specific criteria. Think of it like a search engine for stocks - you tell it what you're looking for, and it shows you companies that match.

Each screener below teaches you how a famous investor thinks about finding opportunities. Remember: these are for educational purposes only!

Try Our Screeners

🎯

Buffett Screener

Find "wonderful companies at fair prices"

Warren Buffett's approach to value investing focuses on companies with durable competitive advantages, strong financials, and honest management.

Key Criteria:

  • Strong free cash flow
  • Low debt-to-equity ratio
  • Consistent ROE > 15%
  • Sustainable competitive advantages
  • Honest, capable management

Graham Screener

Find bargains with a "margin of safety"

Benjamin Graham, the father of value investing, taught Warren Buffett to buy stocks trading below their intrinsic value.

Key Criteria:

  • Low P/E ratio (< 15)
  • P/B ratio < 1.5
  • Dividend yield > 3%
  • Strong balance sheet
  • Current ratio > 2
Try Graham Screener
🚀

Lynch Screener

Find growth at a reasonable price

Peter Lynch's "invest in what you know" philosophy helped him turn $18 million into $14 billion at Fidelity Magellan.

Key Criteria:

  • Earnings growth > 20%
  • Reasonable PEG ratio
  • Understandable business
  • Strong competitive position
  • Insider ownership
Try Lynch Screener
⚡

Icahn Screener

Find undervalued companies with hidden potential

Carl Icahn's activist approach seeks companies trading below their asset value that could benefit from change.

Key Criteria:

  • Trading below book value
  • Potential for restructuring
  • Strong cash position
  • Underperforming but fixable
  • Asset-rich operations
Try Icahn Screener
🧠

Munger Screener

Find owner-operator wonders with durable advantages

Charlie Munger, Buffett's business partner, believed in buying a few truly wonderful businesses and holding them for decades. His approach emphasizes quality over price.

Key Criteria:

  • ROE > 20% consistently
  • Low debt-to-equity ratio
  • Positive free cash flow
  • Owner-operator management
  • Durable competitive advantages
Try Munger Screener
🪄

Greenblatt Screener

The Magic Formula: great companies at great prices

Joel Greenblatt's Magic Formula ranks companies by earnings yield and return on capital — a simple, mechanical system that beats the S&P 500 most years.

Key Criteria:

  • Earnings yield (EBIT/EV) > 10%
  • Return on capital (EBIT/NWC) > 15%
  • Positive net working capital
  • Top-ranked in combined score
  • Diversified basket of 20-30 stocks
Try Greenblatt Screener
📈

O'Neil Screener

CAN SLIM: buy strength, sell weakness

William O'Neil's CAN SLIM system combines fundamentals and technicals to find leading stocks at the moment they're about to break out. Used to find Apple, Microsoft, and NVIDIA.

Key Criteria:

  • Quarterly EPS growth > 25%
  • Annual EPS growth > 25%
  • ROE > 17%
  • Relative strength rating > 80
  • Rising volume trend
